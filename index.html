About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Iowa State University with interests in VLSI, digital hardware, embedded systems, and semiconductor design. This portfolio highlights engineering projects and experiences from my coursework, internships, and senior design project.

Career Objective

After graduating from Iowa State, I hope to begin my career in electrical engineering working with hardware and electronic systems. I am especially interested in digital design, VLSI, and embedded systems because I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software and understanding how a system operates at a low level.

Early in my career, my goal is to continue developing my technical skills while gaining experience working on larger engineering projects. Over time, I would like to take on more responsibility in the design process and contribute to products and systems that require both strong technical knowledge and collaboration across different engineering disciplines.

Internship Experience

Sandisk

Systems Design Engineer Intern

May 2026 – August 2026 | Milpitas, CA

Duties & Projects

During my internship at Sandisk, I worked on storage benchmarking for artificial intelligence workloads. I conducted MLPerf Storage benchmarking for AI Training, KV Cache, and VectorDB workloads to evaluate SSD behavior under representative AI training, inference, and retrieval access patterns.

My work included analyzing storage throughput, latency, accelerator utilization, and Recall@10 metrics. I also investigated filesystem and I/O path tradeoffs between ext4 and XFS configurations and evaluated SSD-backed vector search using DiskANN and HNSW.

Skills & Knowledge Gained

This experience strengthened my understanding of computer hardware, storage systems, benchmarking, and the relationship between software workloads and hardware performance. I also gained experience analyzing large amounts of performance data, troubleshooting system bottlenecks, communicating technical results, and developing engineering recommendations from experimental results.

Burns & McDonnell

Electrical Engineering Intern

May 2025 – August 2025 | Kansas City, MO

Duties & Projects

As an Electrical Engineering Intern at Burns & McDonnell, I assisted with the design and drafting of electrical systems for airports, military bases, and federal facilities. My work included power distribution, lighting, and communication systems.

I created and updated electrical layouts, one-line diagrams, and panelboard schedules. I also performed load calculations and equipment sizing while working with standards and codes including the NEC, NFPA, and UFC.

Skills & Knowledge Gained

This internship gave me experience applying electrical engineering concepts to large-scale real-world projects. I developed technical skills in electrical system design, engineering drawings, load calculations, equipment sizing, and interpreting electrical codes. I also strengthened my communication, teamwork, organization, and ability to work with engineers from multiple disciplines.
