About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Iowa State University with an interest in digital design, VLSI, embedded systems, and hardware development. Through my coursework, engineering projects, and internships, I have gained experience working with both hardware and software systems.

Career Objective

After graduating from Iowa State, I hope to begin my career in electrical engineering working with hardware and electronic systems. I am especially interested in digital design, VLSI, and embedded systems because I enjoy understanding how systems operate at a low level.

Early in my career, my goal is to continue developing my technical skills while gaining experience working on larger engineering projects. Over time, I would like to take on more responsibility in the design process and contribute to products and systems that require both technical knowledge and collaboration between different engineering disciplines. This would most likely lead into a management role where I can be in a more people-facing position.
