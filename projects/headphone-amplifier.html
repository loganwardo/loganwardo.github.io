Analog Circuit Design

Two-Channel Headphone Amplifier

EE 2300 – Electronic Circuits and Systems

Operational Amplifiers • Active Filters • Circuit Analysis • Simulation

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design, simulate, build, and test a two-channel headphone amplifier. The amplifier required separate left and right audio channels with independent volume control. Each channel used an active bandpass filter to provide the required amplification while limiting the frequency response to the specified range.

Design Requirements

  • Two independent audio channels
  • Independent potentiometer volume control for each channel
  • Passband gain of 20 V/V
  • Lower corner frequency of 100 Hz
  • Upper corner frequency of 15 kHz
  • Operational-amplifier-based bandpass filtering

My Role

I worked with my lab partner to design, construct, and test the amplifier. My responsibilities included analyzing the circuit requirements, determining component values for the bandpass filters, constructing the circuit, evaluating simulation and experimental results, and troubleshooting the design during development.

Design & Implementation

We determined that an active bandpass filter was appropriate for each audio channel because the amplifier needed both a specified gain and defined lower and upper corner frequencies. The center frequency was determined from the required frequency range, and resistor and capacitor values were selected based on the desired gain and cutoff frequencies.

The final circuit used an LMC660 operational amplifier. Two amplifier channels were implemented, with one channel controlling the left audio signal and the other controlling the right. Potentiometers configured with voltage dividers provided independent volume adjustment for each channel.

Results

After constructing and testing the amplifier, the circuit successfully met the project requirements. The measured frequency response and gain were close to the target design values.

Parameter Target Measured
Lower Corner Frequency 100 Hz 99.6 Hz
Upper Corner Frequency 15 kHz 15.6 kHz
Passband Gain 20 V/V 20.26 V/V

The completed circuit was also tested using a speaker, allowing us to verify that the amplifier operated successfully with an actual audio output.

Skills & Knowledge Gained

This project strengthened my understanding of operational amplifiers, active filters, frequency response, gain, and practical circuit construction. It also gave me experience translating theoretical circuit requirements into component values and then comparing simulated and measured performance.

The project also improved my troubleshooting and teamwork skills. Designing a circuit that worked both theoretically and physically required us to diagnose problems, reconsider design decisions, and verify our results through measurement.
