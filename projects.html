Below are several projects I have completed as part of my Electrical Engineering coursework at Iowa State University.

Two-Channel Headphone Amplifier

EE 2300 – Electronic Circuits and Systems

Designed, simulated, built, and tested a two-channel headphone amplifier using active bandpass filters and operational amplifiers. The circuit included independent volume control for the left and right audio channels.

PTAT Temperature Sensor

EE 3300 – Integrated Electronics

Designed and laid out a temperature sensor using proportional-to-absolute-temperature behavior, current mirrors, differential amplification, and output scaling. The circuit produced an approximately linear output from 0 to 1 V over a temperature range of 0 to 50°C.

HarvestBot

CPRE 2880 – Embedded Systems

Developed an embedded robotic system that used infrared and ultrasonic sensors, servo control, UART communication, and robot movement. The robot could perform environmental scans and transmit sensor measurements to a graphical interface.
